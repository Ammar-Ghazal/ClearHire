# Demo employers and real job postings

25 real job postings: 20 possible technical skill-overlap examples and five out-of-domain controls. These are research fixtures, not employer accounts or applications. Posting availability changes; recheck before presenting.

Candidate names are preliminary subject-area overlap hypotheses based on profile descriptions, not suitability determinations. Experience, location, work authorization and full requirements were not assessed. Missing evidence does not establish that a person lacks a skill or qualification. No candidate-employer relationship is implied by a hypothesis.

Meta’s original careers pages restricted automated access. Its five listings are supported by indexed third-party records linked below; active status is unconfirmed. Netflix’s official careers page is dynamic; its role details were available in the official search index, so recheck in a browser. The FYZICAL listing encountered a temporary fetch error during final checking.

The five non-software controls are outside ClearHire’s proposed software skill catalogue. The matcher should flag them as out of scope or insufficient evidence, not assign a confident low compatibility score.
